Essential cookies and local storage support security, navigation, account sessions, consent records, and saved preferences. These technologies are necessary for requested functions and may not work correctly if blocked.

The website stores your cookie choice in your browser so the notice does not appear on every visit. Removing browser storage may reset that choice.

Providers supporting hosting, security, analytics, payments, or embedded services may set technologies under their own policies when those features are used. CrimVid does not activate optional categories before consent where applicable.

Your choices

You can accept optional technologies, use essential technologies only, or manage storage through your browser. Blocking essential technologies may prevent sign-in, preferences, or other requested features from working.
