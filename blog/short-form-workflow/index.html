CrimVid field guide

The short-form video workflow for lean teams

A practical operating system for teams that need consistent output without a large editing department.

Record with repurposing in mind

Use clean audio, stable framing, and explicit transitions between topics. A clear source recording produces better edits and fewer review rounds.

Separate selection from polish

First decide which moments deserve to exist. Then refine pacing, framing, captions, and brand treatment. Mixing both decisions too early slows the entire batch.

Create one review checkpoint

Collect transcript, cut, caption, and framing feedback in a single pass. Assign one final approver and define what counts as a blocking issue before review begins.

Export with a naming system

Include source, topic, aspect ratio, and version in each filename. Predictable names make scheduling, revision, and performance analysis far easier.

Close the loop

Review retention and completion data after publishing. Feed those observations back into hook selection and recording structure for the next batch.

Put the workflow into practice

CrimVid helps you move from source recording to platform-ready clips while keeping every editorial decision reviewable.
