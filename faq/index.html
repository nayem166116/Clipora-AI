FAQ

Straight answers before your first upload.

Learn how Clipora approaches source videos, editing control, exports, and security.

What videos work best?

Podcasts, interviews, webinars, tutorials, customer conversations, and talking-head recordings with clear speech work especially well.

Can I edit the AI suggestions?

Yes. Clipora keeps the transcript, cut boundaries, captions, and framing editable before export.

Which formats can I export?

You can prepare vertical 9:16, square 1:1, and landscape 16:9 versions for common social and video platforms.

Does Clipora publish automatically?

Clipora focuses on creating and packaging finished videos. Publishing integrations may vary by plan and availability.

Do I retain ownership of my content?

Yes. You retain ownership of the source files you upload and the outputs created from them, subject to the Terms of Service.
