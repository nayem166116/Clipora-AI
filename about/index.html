About Clipora

The best moments should not stay buried in long videos.

Clipora is building a clearer, faster way for creators and teams to turn recorded knowledge into useful, watchable content.

Our belief

AI should remove repetitive editing work without removing editorial judgment.

Clarity over volume

More clips are not the goal. More useful clips are.

Control by default

Every transcript, cut, caption, and crop stays reviewable.

Why Clipora existsRecorded knowledge should keep working
Our starting point

The hard part is not recording. It is finishing.

Teams already create valuable conversations in podcasts, webinars, interviews, calls, and events. Most of that material is published once—or never—because editing each channel version takes too long.

Clipora is built to make the repetitive decisions faster while preserving human control over context, voice, and quality.

Product principles

How we decide what belongs in Clipora.

Complete ideas over viral fragments

A clip should reward attention, not only capture it. Selection must preserve enough context to be useful.

Human approval before distribution

AI can propose a cut, but the person responsible for the message should approve what leaves the project.

One workflow over scattered tools

Transcript, cut, caption, framing, feedback, and export should stay connected.

Clarity over decorative automation

Every automated decision should be visible, understandable, and editable.

The company we are building

A focused video workflow for teams that publish often.

Today

AI-assisted selection, transcript editing, captions, reframing, review, and platform-ready export.

Next

Deeper publishing workflows, reusable editorial rules, and clearer performance feedback.

Always

Control for the people whose brand, voice, and audience are represented in every cut.
